# Release notes · Auto (mbauto.online)

Newest first. One entry per release, in plain words a user would understand. The full history is also in the app under Settings → Release notes.

## 1.1 · 2026-10-14
- About card at the bottom of Settings: version, release date, links and copyright in one place.
- Installing from matthewbroyer.com: the hub's Install button opens the app with a short install prompt.
- New "page not found" page that matches the app and links back to it.
- Version numbers restart at 1.x with the suite's "Official Web App" label; earlier releases were numbered by build (1.0 to 1.3; the "version 1.2" and "version 1.3" entries below are build numbers).

## 2026-10-14 · version 1.3

- New layout: sections are in a sidebar on computers, a slim rail on tablets, and a bottom bar with **More** on phones.
- The logo and name always take you back to Overview. Browser tabs read "Auto | Fuel" and so on.
- **Share** a vehicle log or fuel economy image from Overview (with one vehicle selected) or Fuel. It's made on your device; the vehicle name and money amounts stay off unless you turn them on.
- Install card in Settings. Once installed, the app is called **Auto**.
- Refreshed look to match the rest of the MB app family: a softer gauge icon, a banner on Overview and light color gradients. Your records didn't change.

## 2026-10-14 · version 1.2

- Receipts and photos on records, Glovebox, History timeline and printable history report, problems and warning lights, repair quotes, cost forecast, keep/repair/replace calculator, inspection checklists, CSV import, install as an app. Details in the app.

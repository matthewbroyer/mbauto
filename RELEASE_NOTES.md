# Release notes: mbfishing.online

The same notes show in the app under Menu → What’s new. Newest first.

## 1.5 · 2026-10-14
- On computers, the search box, backup status and logo spacing now look exactly like the other mb apps: same size, same "Search…" text and the same "/" shortcut.
- The top bar now matches the other mb apps. Search, Data and the light/dark switch are always in the bar, on phones too.
- New Data button: save a backup, back up everything with photos, import, or jump to all backup options. A small dot on it means a backup is due.
- The ≡ menu keeps Help, What’s new and Privacy.
- On the Log screen your current lure now sits next to Start a trip, so there’s no extra line under it.
- Your catches, spots, photos and backups are unchanged.

## 1.4 · 2026-10-14
- New logo, redrawn in the same style as the other mb apps (app icon, tab icon, share image and pages all updated).

- About card at the bottom of More (the app's Settings page): version, release date, links and copyright in one place.
- Installing from matthewbroyer.com: the hub's Install button opens the app with a short install prompt.
- New "page not found" page that matches the app and links back to it.
- The Install card moved to the bottom of More, just above the About card.
- Privacy and Terms pages now end with a link to matthewbroyer.com.

## v1.3 · October 13, 2026

- Navigation moved to a sidebar on the left on computers (a slim icon rail on tablets). Phones keep a bottom bar: Log, Catches, Map, Stats and More. The Species guide is at the top of More on phones
- Updated logo and app icons, with a matching link preview image. If you installed the app, your device may keep the old icon until you reinstall it
- A new fish-and-water banner on the Log screen
- Install card in More now says “Installed on this device” when you are using the installed app
- New share images, made on your device in the app’s colors with the new logo: share a trip, a catch or personal best, or your species count. Preview first, then Share or Save image. They never include your spot, location or map
- The installed app is now named Fishing
- Your catches, spots, photos and backups are unchanged

## v1.2 · October 2026

- Lighter, fresher look (Paper Cutouts): a soft blue tint across the top of each page, a gradient accent line and tab marker, gradient buttons and progress bars, and a leaf-art banner on the Log screen. Layout and features are unchanged.
- New logo and app icons: the same fish on a softer gradient badge, with a matching link preview image.
- Your catches, spots and backups are unchanged.

## v1.1 · October 2026

- Logo redrawn with the fish exactly centered in the app icon, favicon, home-screen icons and link preview image
- More space between the site name and the “Official Web App” line in the header

## v1.11.5 · October 2026

- New logo and app icon: a gold fishing hook on a green gradient, with a matching link preview image
- The logo in the header now has a one-line description under the site name, and tapping it takes you back to the Log screen

## v1.11.3 · October 2026

- New logo and app icon: a new fish facing left on a richer green gradient, with a full outline and dorsal fin, centered with equal margins
- The link preview image now uses the same fish and gradient so the logo, icons and preview match

## v1.11.1 · October 2026

- All pixel art redrawn in 16-bit style: smoother shading, highlights and colored outlines on every fish, plus a new logo, app icon, weather icons and share card
- Colors and markings checked against real photos for all fish, with fixes for many species
- The Species guide is now fish only: the turtle, snake, frog, bird, mammal and crab entries were removed. Anything you already logged under those names is kept

## v1.11.0 · October 2026

- Bite forecast now includes tides for salt water: next highs and lows, a tide line under the chart, and moving water scores better than slack
- Hover (or tap, or use the arrow keys) over any bar in the forecast chart to read what that hour looks like
- New logo and app icons with the fish exactly centered

## v1.10.0 · October 2026

- New bite forecast on the Log screen: a Poor to Great rating for right now and the next 48 hours, with the next good window, for fresh or salt water
- It’s an estimate from weather, daylight and the moon. It doesn’t include tides or water conditions. The forecast loads only when you tap it

## v1.9.0 · October 2026

- Search on the Log screen now finds any species in the guide, even if it isn’t in your region or list
- 56 new species: 24 more fish plus turtles, snakes, frogs, birds, otters, crabs and other wildlife you might see on the water. Fish still come first
- Every species page now has the same sections and source links, and many older pages gained size, range and season info
- The Species page shows how many species you’ve logged

## v1.8.0 · October 2026

- New 8-bit logo and app icon
- Share trip card is now pixel art: your fish and how many of each, the totals, and a short weather line
- Link previews (Messages, Slack, social) show a new pixel-art image

## v1.7.1 · October 2026

- Species drawings are now 8-bit pixel art, with colors and markings corrected for many fish
- New 8-bit app icon and favicon (reinstall the app to the home screen to see the new icon)

## v1.7.0 · October 2026

- Now called Fishing: the installed app name and every browser tab read “Fishing | page name”

- Pick your region (More → Settings, or when you first open the app) and Log shows the fish caught where you fish
- Drawings are simplified illustrations, not photos, and the guide works offline

## v1.6.0 · October 2026

- New look: a compact, desktop-style layout with square panels, a chartreuse accent and almost no animation

- A “Backed up last” line sits above the backup buttons and shows when, what and where
- Opens in light mode every time; dark mode is remembered only if you pick it
